Structure for the dynamic you've agreed on.

Fealty keeps the tasks, rituals, rules and rewards of a power-exchange relationship in one private place you both share.

Open Fealty on the web

Android app coming soon.

Tasks and rituals

One-off or recurring on any pattern, with ordered steps for rituals. Ask for more time instead of missing one.

Rules and protocols

The rules you live by, the specifics that spell them out, and a log for when one slips.

Rewards and consequences

Points for what gets done, rewards to spend them on, and consequences that come due when something's missed.

Your agreement and limits

Write down what you've agreed and both sign it. Rate your limits side by side, seen only once you've both finished.

Check-ins and a journal

A mood with a task when you want one, a journal that can stay yours alone, and a look back together each month.

Scenes and aftercare

Log a scene, keep each other's aftercare needs close, and check in the next day.

Places and curfew

Tasks done by getting somewhere, and a home-by time. The Android app checks only the places you've named.

Proof when it's wanted

Photos seen only by the people in your dynamic, blurred until tapped, and deleted on a schedule you choose or when the dynamic ends.

A safe word in reach

For the submissive, one tap from any screen. It stays up until whoever called it clears it.

Discreet by design

Notifications stay vague unless you choose otherwise. On Android, lock the app with your fingerprint and give it an ordinary icon and name.

Fealty is for adults, 18 and over, in relationships everyone involved has freely agreed to.
