Fealty keeps the tasks, rituals, rules and rewards of a power-exchange relationship in one private place you both share.

Android app coming soon.

Tasks and rituals

One-off or recurring on any pattern, with ordered steps for rituals. Ask to reschedule instead of missing one.

Rules and protocols

Write down what you've agreed, log how it's going, and keep limits where you can both see them.

Rewards and consequences

Points for what gets done, and the rewards and consequences you've set together.

Notes and journal

Shared notes for the two of you, and a journal that can stay yours alone.

Proof when it's wanted

Photos seen only by the people in your dynamic, blurred until tapped, and deleted when the dynamic ends.

Discreet by design

Notifications never name the task. On Android, lock the app with your fingerprint and give it an ordinary icon and name.

Fealty is for adults, 18 and over, in relationships everyone involved has freely agreed to.
